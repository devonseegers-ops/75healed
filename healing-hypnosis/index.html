Why this works

Hypnosis is not a party trick.
It is one of the most studied things here.

This is the same principle as the rest of #75HEALED. Not willpower. Not forcing yourself to feel positive about something that hurts. A specific, repeatable input that changes what the nervous system is doing — measured in operating rooms and sleep labs, not just felt.

Clinical hypnosis is a focused attentional state with heightened response to suggestion. That is all it is. You are awake, you are in control, and you can stop at any point. Here is some of what it has been shown to do.

Brief hypnosis before breast surgery
Montgomery et al. · J Natl Cancer Inst · 2007

200 patients randomised to a 15-minute presurgery hypnosis session or attention control. The hypnosis group reported less pain, nausea, fatigue, discomfort and emotional upset, needed less propofol and lidocaine, and cost the institution $772.71 less per patient.

Self-hypnotic relaxation during invasive procedures
Lang et al. · The Lancet · 2000

241 patients undergoing vascular and renal procedures. Self-hypnotic relaxation reduced pain and anxiety, improved haemodynamic stability, and cut procedure time from 78 minutes to 61.

Hypnosis as a treatment for anxiety
Valentine et al. · Int J Clin Exp Hypn · 2019

Meta-analysis of 17 trials. The average person receiving hypnosis reduced anxiety more than about 79% of controls, rising to 84% at longest follow-up. It worked better alongside other approaches than alone.

Hypnosis and sleep outcomes
Chamine, Atchley & Oken · J Clin Sleep Med · 2018

Systematic review of 24 studies. 14 found hypnosis beneficial for sleep, and the effect held when only lower-risk-of-bias studies were analysed.

Why the 5.5 seconds matters
Lehrer & Gevirtz · Front Psychol · 2014

Breathing near six breaths a minute hits the body's resonance frequency, where heart rate and breath synchronise and baroreflex function strengthens. A 5.5 second inhale and 5.5 second exhale is that rate. It is the reason for the chime.

This is a self-guided audio, not treatment, and not a replacement for a clinician. If you are dealing with something serious — psychosis, severe trauma, anything you are being treated for — bring a professional into it. Don't listen while driving.
